Let $X$ be a compact Kähler manifold. The Hodge decomposition expresses each de Rham cohomology group as a direct sum of Dolbeault cohomology groups:

$$
H^k(X,\mathbb{C}) \simeq \bigoplus_{p+q=k} H^{p,q}(X).
$$

In particular, the dimensions satisfy $b_k=\sum_{p+q=k}h^{p,q}$, where $b_k$ is the $k$-th Betti number and $h^{p,q}=\dim_{\mathbb{C}}H^{p,q}(X)$.
