In the second semester of my fourth undergraduate year, I took MATH4080 Modules and Group Representation, taught by Prof. Jiu Kang Yu. The main topics were:

1. The classification theorem for finitely generated modules over a PID, together with its proof and applications.
2. Representations of finite groups. (The approximate scope was the group representation theory portion of Artin's *Algebra* and Chapters 1–6 of Serre's *Linear Representations of Finite Groups*.)

The material was largely standard, but the course approached it from the viewpoint of G-modules. It is suitable as a supplement to standard textbooks or for a second study of the subject. These notes are adapted from the lecture handouts.
