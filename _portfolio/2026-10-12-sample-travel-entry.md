*This is a sample travel entry. Replace it with your own writing and photographs.*

An early walk can reveal a different rhythm of a city: shop shutters still closed, side streets almost empty, and temple grounds just beginning to fill with sound. A travel note can be as short as a single observation, or grow into a longer essay with a map and photographs.

To add a photograph, place the image in the `images` folder and reference it from this entry.
