These are my essays from UGEA2330 *China's Cultural Heritage*, a Spring 2026 course at CUHK taught by Prof. Lam Weng Cheong. Introducing Chinese cultural heritage from the perspective of heritage studies, the course had us study the UNESCO documents on heritage designation and critically examine the items listed in them, while looking closely at the relations between heritage and local communities, the relations between heritage and history and historical research, and the problems of heritage conservation and development.

The TA of the course, Xiao Yiyang, also helped me a great deal: she introduced me to the treatment of difficult heritage and sparked my interest in colonial heritage, which my final paper explores.
