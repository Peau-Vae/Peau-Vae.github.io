In August 2026, together with Wong Pui Chik and Liu Yichen, I organized a seminar on spectral sequences. Our references were:

- Bott and Tu, *Differential Forms in Algebraic Topology*, Chapter III, Sections 14–16;
- John McCleary, *A User's Guide to Spectral Sequences*;
- Silvio Barandun, *Leray–Serre Spectral Sequences: A Morse Homological Approach*;
- Alexander Ritter, *Cambridge Part III Course on Morse Homology*, 2011.

In the seminar we reviewed the definition of spectral sequences in its two equivalent formulations (via subquotients of a filtration and via exact couples), and proved the Leray–Serre theorem in the frameworks of de Rham cohomology, singular cohomology and Morse (co)homology.
