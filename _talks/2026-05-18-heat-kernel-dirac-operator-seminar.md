Together with Zhou Tianyuan and Wong Pui Chik, I organized the IMS Students Geometry Seminar on heat kernels and the local index theorem, held at IMS 502a, CUHK from May 18 to July 30, 2026. Our main reference was Berline, Getzler and Vergne, *Heat Kernels and Dirac Operators*.
